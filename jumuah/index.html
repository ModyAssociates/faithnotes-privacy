Jumuah Journal privacy and data notice

Effective 13 November 2026

Jumuah Journal is an Android app published by Fun Fat Studios. It is a quiet place to write down what you remember after Jumuah and to keep a hifz routine steady: you write, you type references such as “2:255” or “Al-Baqarah 255”, and the ayah opens from the Quran bundled inside the app. This notice describes exactly what the app does with your information, and what it does not do.

The short version. No account, no tracking, no server of ours. Your khutbah notes, ayah notes, highlights and hifz progress stay in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit or sell:

  • the notes you write, the tags you add, or the references you link;
  • your highlights, ayah notes, bookmarks or reading history;
  • your hifz plans, segments, logs or mistakes;
  • which surah you read, search for, or look up;
  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts;
  • your location;
  • advertising identifiers — and we show no advertising.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. Nothing about your worship, your reading or your writing leaves the device.

Why the design is this simple

A record of who reads the Quran, or of what someone is memorising, can reveal religious belief — a special category of personal data under many privacy laws, and in some places a risk to the person holding it. The simplest safe design is the one we chose: there is no server to send your data to.

The Quran text (Tanzil Uthmani) and the English meaning of Pickthall (1930) are bundled inside the app, not downloaded. The approximate Hijri date is computed on the device; no calendar service is contacted.

Backups

  • Android Auto Backup may copy your local database to your own Google account if you have it switched on for your device. That transfer is handled by Google under Google's terms. The bundled scripture text is excluded because it ships with the app.
  • Manual backups are files you create and choose a destination for, using the Android share sheet. If you set a password, the file is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-SHA256); we cannot recover that password.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment card or billing address. Google returns a purchase token, product id, purchase time and purchase state to the app so it can grant and restore the unlock; that is stored only on your device. We have no server to receive it.

Permissions

  • Notifications — asked only when you turn on a reminder.
  • Biometric or device credential — used only for the optional app lock, and never leaves the device.
  • Internet — used only by Google Play Billing. Reading, search, notes and hifz all work in airplane mode.

Children

Jumuah Journal is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not part of the Google Play Families programme.

Your choices

  • Delete a note — it moves to Trash for 30 days, then is purged.
  • Delete all data — Settings → “Delete all data” removes every note, tag, highlight, plan and setting from the device.
  • Uninstall — removes all app data from the device.

A Play purchase is held by Google in your Google account and is restored if you install the app again. We cannot delete that record.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated text ships in the app and the date above changes. Because there is nothing to notify you by — we hold no contact details for you — the in-app text is always the current version.
