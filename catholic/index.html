Missal privacy and data notice

Effective 14 November 2026

Missal is an Android app published by Fun Fat Studios. It is a quiet notebook for homilies and Bible study: you write, you type references such as “Tobit 12:7”, and the verse opens from text bundled inside the app. This notice describes exactly what Missal does with your information, and what it does not do.

The short version. No account, no tracking, no server of ours. Your notes, highlights and reading history stay in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit or sell:

  • the notes you write, the tags you add, or the references you link;
  • your highlights, verse notes, bookmarks or reading history;
  • which scripture you read, search for, or look up;
  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts;
  • your location;
  • advertising identifiers — and we show no advertising.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. Nothing about your reading or your writing leaves the device.

Why the design is this simple

Notes about homilies and what you believe reveal religious belief, which many privacy laws treat as a special category of personal data. The simplest safe design is the one we chose: there is no server to send your data to.

The Bible text (Douay-Rheims 1899 and the World English Bible Catholic edition) and its search index are bundled inside the app, not downloaded.

Backups

  • Android Auto Backup may copy your local database to your own Google account if you have it switched on for your device. That transfer is handled by Google under Google's terms. The bundled scripture text is excluded because it ships with the app.
  • Manual backups are files you create and choose a destination for, using the Android share sheet. If you set a password, the file is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-SHA256); we cannot recover that password.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment card or billing address. Google returns a purchase token, product id, purchase time and purchase state to the app so it can grant and restore the unlock; that is stored only on your device. We have no server to receive it.

Permissions

  • Notifications — asked only when you turn on a reminder.
  • Biometric or device credential — used only for the optional app lock, and never leaves the device.
  • Internet — used only by Google Play Billing. Every other feature works in airplane mode.

Children

Missal is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not part of the Google Play Families programme.

Your choices

  • Delete a note — it moves to Trash for 30 days, then is purged.
  • Delete all data — Settings → “Delete all data” removes every note, tag, highlight and setting from the device.
  • Uninstall — removes all app data from the device.

A Play purchase is held by Google in your Google account and is restored if you install the app again. We cannot delete that record.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated text ships in the app and the date above changes. Because there is nothing to notify you by — we hold no contact details for you — the in-app text is always the current version.
